## Finished

### Deep Work

Worth it for the framing alone: treating focus as a skill you train rather than a mood you wait for. The scheduling advice is harder to apply with meetings scattered through the day.

### Range

Argues generalists win in "wicked" domains where feedback is slow and patterns don't repeat. A good counterweight to the 10,000-hours narrative.
