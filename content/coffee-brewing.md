## The golden ratio

Start at **1:16** coffee to water by weight and adjust to taste. Stronger? Go 1:15. Brighter and lighter? Try 1:17.

## Pour-over (V60)

- 20g coffee, 320g water at 94°C
- 45 second bloom with 60g water
- Finish pouring by 2:30, drawdown done by 3:30
- Medium-fine grind, like table salt

## French press

- 30g coffee, 480g water
- 4 minute steep, then press slowly
- Coarse grind — fines make it muddy

## Espresso

| Variable | Target |
| -------- | ------ |
| Dose     | 18g |
| Yield    | 36g (1:2) |
| Time     | 25–30s |
| Temp     | 93°C |

If it's sour, grind finer or raise temp. If it's bitter, grind coarser or lower temp.

## Notes to self

- Buy whole beans, grind right before brewing
- Water matters more than expected — filtered, not distilled
- Roast date within the last month, rest beans a week after roasting
